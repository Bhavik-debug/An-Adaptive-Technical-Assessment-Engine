### Read this before any number below: the grader and the reference share a model

The configured provider serves every capability tier from one model, so three
roles that should be independent are filled by the same weights:

- answers **written** by: `nvidia/nemotron-3.5-lightning-30b-a3b`
- answers **graded** by: `nvidia/nemotron-3.5-lightning-30b-a3b`
- reference labels **assigned** by: `nvidia/nemotron-3.5-lightning-30b-a3b`

That makes the Day-19 reference comparison **partly circular**: it measures
agreement between two different prompts to one model, and a shared blind spot is
invisible to it. The Day-18 label passes were produced by a *different* model
family (`claude-opus-5`), so despite their own contamination they are the **less
circular** reference for grader validity - which is why all three are reported
side by side below rather than one being promoted to primary.

Neither circularity nor contamination is fixable with the infrastructure
available; both are reported.

**The confidence gate is firing on 32% of answers against the ~8% plan section 7.3 budgets for.** Breakdown by trigger: `signal_disagreement` 39.

`signal_disagreement` means the concept score and the rubric score differed by more than 0.25 - the checklist and the rubric told different stories about the same answer. At this rate that is a statement about the prompt rather than about a handful of hard items, and it is the direct cause of the 1.93 calls per answer: every gated answer costs four.

**The evidence verifier downgraded a span on 46% of answers.** Each one is a span the model quoted as proof that a concept was covered, which did not appear in the answer. Plan section 7.4 ranks substring verification second among the reliability techniques; this is how much it is doing. Without it those concepts would have scored as covered on the strength of quotations that do not exist.

## Test-retest reliability

- items = **6**, runs each = **5**
- mean sigma = **0.03861** (target < 0.04) **PASS**
- max sigma on any one item = **0.07061**
- items whose score varied at all = **6/6**
- largest spread on one item = **0.1956** score units
- items where the confidence gate fired at least once = **2**

The gate is the expected source of variance: a gated score is the median of three temperature-0.7 re-grades (plan section 7.3), so it is designed to vary. An ungated grade runs at temperature 0 and should be stable.

Items at other run depths were **excluded** rather than truncated into this sigma: 1 item(s) with 2 runs, 1 item(s) with 4 runs. Mixing depths would report a sigma computed over the shallowest group under the deepest group's label.
